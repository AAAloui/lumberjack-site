Delete your account

This permanently deletes your Lumberjack account: your workouts, routines, records, synced backup and your sign-in. It can't be undone. It's the same as Profile → Delete account in the app, and you don't need the app installed.

A subscription isn't cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. Data that only exists on a phone where the app is still installed is removed when you delete the account in the app or uninstall it.

1. Sign in to confirm it's you
2. Delete

Type DELETE to confirm.
