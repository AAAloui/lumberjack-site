Delete your account

This permanently deletes your Lumberjack account: your workouts, routines, records, synced backup and your sign-in. It can't be undone. It's the same as Profile → Delete account in the app, and you don't need the app installed.

A subscription isn't cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

Phones that still have the app

  • Deleting here removes your synced data and your sign-in right away. A phone that still has the app keeps its local copy until it is online again.
  • The next time that phone is online (when you open the app, return to it, or sign in), the app checks whether the account still exists. If it doesn't, the app removes that account's workouts, routines, records and settings from the phone, signs out, and shows "This account was deleted."
  • While the phone is offline, you can still sign in there and see the local copy. Nothing is removed until the phone is back online and the check succeeds; if it can't reach our servers, it tries again later.
  • If the app asks you to sign in again, that's how it confirms the account is gone.
  • Signing in again later with the same Google account creates a new, empty account. Deleted data can't be brought back.
