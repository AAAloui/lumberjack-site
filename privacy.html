LUMBERJACK

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Lumberjack ("the app") is a workout tracker for Android phones and Wear OS watches, run by Almostapha A. ALOUI ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who processes it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • AccountUsername, email address, and either a password (stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash on your device; Firebase holds its own credential) or your Google account identifier. Used to sign you in and keep your data tied to your account.
  • Optional profile detailsGender, height, weight, birthday and units, if you enter them. Shown in your profile and used for your own stats.
  • Training dataWorkouts, exercises, sets, routines, programs, rest timers and personal records. The core of the app.
  • Subscription statusSubscription status and trial start date, to know whether your trial is active or you have purchased access.
  • Usage analyticsOnly if you turn on "Share usage analytics" (off by default): app events such as starting a trial or logging a workout, and whether your account is in the early-tester group. Used to understand how the app is used and whether it is worth continuing.
  • Crash reportsDevice model, OS version, app version and the technical details of a crash. Used to find and fix bugs.

On the watch, heart rate is read during a workout to show it on screen. It is never stored or sent anywhere.

The app shows no ads, does not collect the advertising ID, and never sells your data.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Your device. Everything you log is stored on your phone first.
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Firebase Authentication for sign-in, Cloud Firestore for backing up and syncing your data between devices, Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics 4) only with your consent, and Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports.
  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.): to process purchases and keep track of what you bought. RevenueCat receives your account identifier and purchase history, not your training data. Payment details are handled by Google Play and never reach us.
  • Wear OS. Your phone and watch exchange routines and workouts directly over Google's Wearable Data Layer.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own data protection commitments.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn usage analytics on or off at any time in Profile.
  • Turn backup and sync off in Profile; your data then stays on your device.
  • Delete your account and all associated data from Profile in the app, or from the web at lumberjack.fit/delete-account. This removes your local data, your synced data in Firestore, and your sign-in account. If you delete it from the web, a phone that still has the app removes its local copy of that account the next time it is online. Purchase records kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for legal and accounting reasons are subject to their own retention.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us at privacy@lumberjack.fit.

Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, synced data and the sign-in account are deleted right away; backups held by our providers expire on their own schedules.

After a deletion we keep a record containing only the deleted account's internal ID and the time of deletion, so your other devices know the account was deleted. It contains no email address or other personal details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when this policy changes, and point out significant changes in the app.
